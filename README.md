**Dapur Nusantara** is a full Indonesian restaurant cooking game. You are the chef of a warm, batik-trimmed kitchen: grab ingredients from the prep counter, chop them on the talenan, fry them in the wok or grill sate over coals, assemble multi-component dishes on plates exactly as the tickets demand, and serve them at the pass before customers lose patience.

A shift lasts 180 seconds across a 3-day campaign. Tips scale with remaining patience, on-time serves build a combo up to x5, VIP orders pay double but wait less, and a mid-shift rush hour doubles the incoming orders. Between shifts you spend earned coins on one upgrade — a sharper knife, a third wok, friendlier service, a second pair of hands, or cooler coals. Seven original recipes unlock as the shift heats up, from Nasi Ayam Kilat to Gado-Gado Nusantara. All art is drawn in canvas, all sounds are synthesized WebAudio — no external assets, no network.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser.

Controls: WASD / arrow keys to move, E or Space to use a station, click a station to walk there and use it, P to pause, M to mute.
